# Deprecation plan

**The problem:** Features get retired with one email and a date. Customers are surprised, support gets flooded on the last day, and key accounts escalate.

**What it does:** Sizes who's affected, builds a timeline from internal prep through end date, drafts a plain-spoken customer announcement, and plans for the customers who won't move in time, including who can approve exceptions.

**Use it with:** what's being retired, why, usage data if available, and the replacement.

**You get:** a deprecation plan with a dated timeline, message drafts, and open questions.
