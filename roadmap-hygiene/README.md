# Roadmap hygiene

**The problem:** Roadmaps fill up with items past their date, without owners, or titled "Phase 2," and people stop trusting them.

**What it does:** Checks every item for stale dates, missing owners or timeframes, vague titles, in-progress items with no linked work, contradictions, zombies, and customer-facing overpromises. Recommends one action per flagged item and never edits the roadmap itself.

**Use it with:** a roadmap export, planning sheet, or a Jira, Linear, or Productboard view.

**You get:** a short cleanup list with customer-facing risks at the top.
