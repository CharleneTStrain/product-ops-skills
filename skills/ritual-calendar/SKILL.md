# Ritual calendar

Every recurring meeting should earn its slot by producing something: a decision, an update people act on, or a fix. A ritual calendar lays the whole rhythm out at once, so gaps and duplicates show up.

## Step 1: List what exists (for an audit)

For each recurring meeting:

| Meeting | Cadence | Length | Attendees | Purpose (as stated) | Output | Owner | Could it be async? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

"Output" is what exists after the meeting that didn't before: a decision log entry, an updated tracker, a recap with action items. If there's no output, write "None." That's a finding.

## Step 2: Check coverage

A product team typically needs a moment for each of these. Check which are covered, by which meeting, and which are missing:

| Need | Typical cadence | Typical output |
|---|---|---|
| Plan the work | Quarterly, with a mid-quarter check | Plan, priorities |
| Decide what's next | Every sprint or cycle | Committed scope |
| See what shipped | Every sprint or cycle | Sprint summary |
| Check product health | Weekly or every two weeks | KPI health check |
| Hear from customers | Weekly or monthly | VoC digest |
| Review launches | Before each launch | Go / no-go readout |
| Improve how we work | After launches and each quarter | Retro changes |
| Share with the wider company | Monthly or quarterly | Readout or update |

## Step 3: Find the problems

- **No purpose or no output**
- **Overlap:** two meetings cover the same need with the same people
- **Wrong cadence:** too often for how fast the thing changes, or too rare to catch problems
- **Wrong people:** decision meetings without the decider; update meetings with people who only need the recap
- **Should be async:** pure status updates that a written post would cover
- **Missing:** a need from Step 2 with no meeting or written ritual

## Step 4: Recommend the rhythm

```
# Team rhythm — [Team] — [Date]

## Weekly
| Ritual | Day | Length | Who | Purpose | Output | Owner |

## Every sprint / cycle
[Same table]

## Monthly
[Same table]

## Quarterly
[Same table]

## Changes from today
- **Cut:** [meeting] — [why; what replaces it]
- **Merge:** [A] + [B] → [new]
- **Move to async:** [meeting] → [written format]
- **Add:** [ritual] — [the gap it fills]

## Total time per person per week
- Before: [hours] · After: [hours]
```

Compute time totals with real arithmetic. Recommend changes; the team decides. Never create, move, or cancel calendar events yourself.
