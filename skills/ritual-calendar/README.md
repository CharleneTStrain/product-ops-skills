# Ritual calendar

**The problem:** Teams collect recurring meetings with no clear purpose or output, while some important moments (like checking product health or hearing from customers) have no slot at all.

**What it does:** Audits every recurring meeting for purpose, output, cadence, and attendees; checks coverage against what a product team needs; and recommends a weekly, sprint, monthly, and quarterly rhythm, with what to cut, merge, move to async, or add, and the time saved.

**Use it with:** a list of recurring meetings or a description of the team's current rhythm.

**You get:** a recommended operating rhythm. It never changes calendar events.
