# New PM onboarding pack

**The problem:** New product hires spend their first month figuring out where things live and who to ask.

**What it does:** Builds a "start here" pack: the five to seven docs that matter most, the product in one page, the area they'll own, tools and access, recurring meetings and their role in each, people to meet and why, a glossary, and a checkable 30/60/90 plan. Missing docs or processes are flagged as gaps.

**Use it with:** whatever exists about the team, product, tools, and meetings.

**You get:** a day-one onboarding pack and a draft 30/60/90 plan for the manager to edit.
