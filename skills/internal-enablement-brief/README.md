# Internal enablement brief

**The problem:** Sales, support, and customer success learn about changes from customers, then improvise answers or overpromise.

**What it does:** Writes a one-page brief with what's changing, who it's for, a line anyone can say to a customer, what not to promise, and a section each for sales, support, and customer success. Undecided items stay clearly separated.

**Use it with:** a launch plan, spec, release notes, or pricing change details.

**You get:** a one-page brief customer-facing teams can use the day it lands.
