# Meeting to action converter

**The problem:** Meetings end with agreement but no owners. A week later, everyone thought someone else was doing it.

**What it does:** Pulls action items, decisions, and open questions out of notes or transcripts. Gives every action one named owner and a due date, keeps "we should" separate from "I will," and flags anything with no owner or no date.

**Use it with:** meeting notes, transcripts, or recording summaries.

**You get:** a short recap ready to paste into chat or email.
