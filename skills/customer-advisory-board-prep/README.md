# Customer advisory board prep

**The problem:** CAB sessions turn into roadmap presentations, members never hear what happened with their input, and they stop showing up.

**What it does:** Sets two or three learning goals only these customers can answer, checks the member list for at-risk accounts, builds an agenda that opens with "what we did with your input last time," writes open discussion questions, and produces a write-up and follow-up tracker.

**Use it with:** the member list, last session's notes, and what the company wants to learn.

**You get:** goals, agenda, questions, a one-page pre-read, and a post-session write-up with commitments.
