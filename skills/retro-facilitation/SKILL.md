# Retro facilitation

A retro is worth the hour only if something changes afterward. Most retros produce a long list of feelings and no owners. This one ends with two or three changes someone has agreed to make.

## Step 1: Set it up

Confirm:
- **What we're looking back on:** the project, launch, sprint, or incident, with dates
- **Who's in the room:** everyone who did the work; leaders only if they won't stifle honesty
- **The goal:** usually "decide what to keep doing and what to change next time"

Send a pre-read with the timeline of what happened (key dates, decisions, and outcomes), so the session doesn't spend 20 minutes reconstructing it.

## Step 2: Run the session (60 minutes)

```
0:00  Set the tone (2 min)
      "We're here to improve how we work, not to find who's at fault."
0:02  Timeline check (8 min)
      Walk the pre-read. Fill in what's missing.
0:10  Silent writing (10 min)
      Everyone writes on their own:
      - What went well that we should keep
      - What got in the way
      - What surprised us
0:20  Share and group (15 min)
      Read out, cluster similar notes, name each cluster.
0:35  Pick what matters (10 min)
      Everyone votes for the 2 or 3 clusters most worth fixing.
0:45  Decide changes (13 min)
      For each top cluster: what will we do differently, who owns it, by when.
0:58  Close (2 min)
      Read back the changes and owners.
```

For incidents, replace "What surprised us" with "Where did we first see a sign, and what stopped us from acting on it?"

## Step 3: Write it up

If notes come in raw, group them into clusters before writing. Keep people's exact words for the notes; don't soften them.

```
# Retro: [What] — [Date]
**Attendees:** [names]

## Keep doing
- [Cluster] — [one line]

## Changes we're making
| Change | Owner | By when | How we'll know it worked |

## Also came up (not acting on now)
- [Cluster] — [why parked]

## Timeline
[Short version from the pre-read]
```

Rules:
- Two or three changes, maximum. Ten changes means none happen.
- Every change is a specific behavior or process, not a value ("communicate better" is not a change; "post launch status in the launch channel every Monday" is).
- No names attached to problems. Name owners only on changes.
- At the next retro, start by checking whether last time's changes happened.
