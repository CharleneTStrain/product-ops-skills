# Voice of customer digest

**The problem:** Feedback gets collected but rarely reaches the people who decide what to build, and when it does, it's a wall of comments nobody reads.

**What it does:** Writes a one-screen digest with a single headline, the top three to five themes ranked by volume and stakes, what changed since last period, short real quotes, and specific things worth a look.

**Use it with:** tagged feedback (pairs with the feedback-tagging skill) and the previous digest, if there is one.

**You get:** a recurring update leadership and product teams can read in two minutes.
