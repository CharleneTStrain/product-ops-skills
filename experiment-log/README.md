# Experiment log

**The problem:** Teams rerun tests someone already ran, or remember a flat result as a win, because nobody wrote down what was tested and what was decided.

**What it does:** Writes an experiment brief before the test (hypothesis, one primary metric, guardrails, what success looks like) and a result entry after (numbers as reported, what was learned, the decision, who made it). Keeps negative and inconclusive results in the log.

**Use it with:** a test idea, an experiment plan, or results from an analysis.

**You get:** consistent log entries and a searchable summary table.
