# Quarterly planning prep

Planning meetings go badly when the first hour is spent figuring out what happened last quarter. The pre-read does that work in advance, so the meeting is spent deciding.

## Step 1: Look back

From the source (last quarter's goals, roadmap, metrics, release notes), record:

| Goal or commitment | Target | Result | Met? | Why, if given |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Then list:
- **Shipped:** the main things that went live
- **Carried over:** planned, not finished, with current status
- **Dropped:** removed on purpose, and why

Use the numbers and reasons in the source. Don't grade work as a success or failure beyond what the targets show.

## Step 2: Gather inputs

Pull together what the team should weigh, each with its source:

- **Business:** company goals, revenue targets, commitments leadership has made
- **Customers:** top themes from feedback and the VoC digest, escalations, renewal risks
- **Sales:** deals blocked on missing capability, with named stakes where given
- **Product health:** KPIs that are off track (see the kpi-health-check skill)
- **Tech:** debt, reliability, or platform work engineering says can't wait
- **Commitments already made:** anything promised to customers, partners, or leadership with a date

Keep each input to one or two lines with evidence. Mark anything that's an opinion as an opinion.

## Step 3: Capacity

Record what the source says about people and time: team size, planned leave, hiring, and how much capacity is already committed to carried-over work, support rotation, or keep-the-lights-on work. If capacity isn't given, flag it as missing. Planning without it is guessing.

## Step 4: Frame the decisions

List the specific choices planning needs to make, not a list of topics:
- "Do we finish X before starting Y?"
- "Which of these three customer asks makes the quarter?"
- "Does tech debt get a fixed share of capacity?"

## Output

```
# [Quarter] planning pre-read — [Team]
**Planning session:** [date] · **Read by:** [date]

## Last quarter in one paragraph
[What we set out to do and what happened.]

## Results
[Table from Step 1]

## Carrying into next quarter
- [Item] — [status]

## Inputs to weigh
### Business · Customers · Sales · Product health · Tech · Commitments
- [Input] — [evidence / source]

## Capacity
- [What's known] · Missing: [what's not]

## Decisions this planning session needs to make
1. [Decision]

## Open questions before the session
- [Question] — ask [name]
```

The pre-read informs the plan; it doesn't propose one. Don't rank priorities unless asked.
