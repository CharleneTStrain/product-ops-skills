# Quarterly planning prep

**The problem:** Planning sessions spend the first hour reconstructing last quarter, and decisions get made without capacity or customer inputs in the room.

**What it does:** Builds a pre-read with last quarter's results against targets, carried-over work, inputs from the business, customers, sales, product health, and engineering, known capacity, and the specific decisions planning needs to make. It informs the plan; it doesn't write it.

**Use it with:** last quarter's goals and roadmap, metrics, release notes, feedback digests, and capacity info.

**You get:** a planning pre-read the team can read before the session.
