# Pricing and packaging change log

**The problem:** A pricing change lands on the website but not in billing, the sales deck, or the help center, and nobody can say what a plan included last year.

**What it does:** Logs every approved change with before and after, effective date, who it applies to, grandfathering, and approver, then tracks it through every place it has to land. Answers "what did this plan include on [date]?" from the log.

**Use it with:** a pricing decision, plan comparison, or record of past changes.

**You get:** a dated change log and a rollout checklist per change. It never touches prices or billing.
