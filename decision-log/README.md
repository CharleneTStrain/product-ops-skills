# Decision log

**The problem:** Decisions get made in meetings and threads, then nobody can say who decided, why, or whether it was decided at all.

**What it does:** Reads notes or threads and separates what was decided from what was only leaning or discussed. Logs each real decision with who, why, what was ruled out, what it affects, and when to revisit. Never fills in reasons the source doesn't give.

**Use it with:** meeting notes, Slack threads, email chains, or transcripts.

**You get:** clean log entries plus a "not yet decided" list with who needs to make each call.
